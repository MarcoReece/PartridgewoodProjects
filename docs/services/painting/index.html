Painting Services

Painting improves a building's look while protecting its surfaces. Our painting service follows a structured process to ensure a durable, high-quality finish:

  1. Surface Preparation

    We pressure-wash the home, repair plaster issues, and sand all wood and metal surfaces to create a stable base that is ready for the final coating.

  2. Primer Application

    Where needed, we apply the correct primer to areas like trim, eaves, gutters, doors, and frames, followed by two coats suited to each surface.

  3. Painting

    Once the surfaces have been properly prepared, the appropriate coating is selected for the interior or exterior surface, including metalwork or wood.

  4. Cleanup

    After the painting is completed, we meticulously clean up the work area. This includes removing paint splatters, packing up equipment, and ensuring your property is left in a clean and tidy condition.

  5. Drying Time

    We allow sufficient time for the paint to dry, ensuring that it cures properly. The drying time varies depending on the type of paint used and environmental conditions.

  6. Inspect and Touch Up

    Once dry, we inspect all painted areas and fix any imperfections to ensure a flawless finish.
